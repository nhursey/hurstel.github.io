About

ClueList is an Android app that reminds you to pick up items from your shopping list when you're near stores that carry them. No more forgetting the milk when you're already at the grocery store.

Features

  • Location-Based Cues — Push notifications when you're near a relevant store
  • Built-in Shopping List — Categories, quantities, notes, swipe-to-delete with undo
  • List Anywhere — Export your list to any notes app on your device
  • Stores Tab — View nearest stores, adjust search distance, exclude stores you don't want
  • 15 Preset Categories — Produce, Dairy, Meat, and more, plus custom entries
  • Light/Dark Theme — Choose your preference

Tech Stack

  • Kotlin + Android SDK (minSdk 24, targetSdk 34)
  • Room Database for local persistence
  • Google Play Services Location for GPS tracking
  • Material Components for UI
  • Patrick Hand custom font
