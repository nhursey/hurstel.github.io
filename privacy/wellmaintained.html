Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 14, 2026
Last updated: July 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Well-Maintained. This Privacy Policy explains how Hurstel ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Well-Maintained does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted Room database.

Data stored locally on your device includes:
• Home profiles (property type, year built, square footage, systems info)
• Maintenance task schedules and completion history
• System and appliance records (brand, model, warranty dates)
• Cost entries for maintenance work (DIY vs professional)

No account creation or sign-in is required. No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers are used.

3. How We Use Information

All data is used solely within the app to:
• Generate personalized maintenance schedules based on your home profile
• Track task completion and send reminders for upcoming/overdue maintenance
• Display your Home Health Score based on maintenance activity
• Log costs associated with maintenance work

No data is shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party. All processing occurs entirely on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Well-Maintained does not currently use any third-party services that process your data. The app uses standard Android APIs (WorkManager, Notifications) that operate entirely on-device. No data is transmitted to any external server.

5. Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device, there is no server-side data retention. Your data remains on your device as long as the app is installed. You can delete all data at any time using the "Clear All Data" option in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

6. Data Security

We are committed to protecting your information. Since all data processing and storage occurs locally on your device using Android's encrypted Room database, no data is transmitted over the network. We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access to your device-level data.

7. Children's Privacy

Well-Maintained does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 18. The app does not collect any information from any age group. All data is generated by the user through on-device app usage.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting. We will notify users of material changes through the app, which may require re-acceptance of updated terms.

9. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Alabama, United States.
