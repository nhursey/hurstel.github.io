Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026
Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ClueList. This Privacy Policy explains how Hurstel ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

ClueList does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data is stored locally on your device.

Data stored locally on your device includes:

  • Shopping list items and categories
  • Store preferences and exclusion settings
  • Theme preference (light/dark)

No account creation or sign-in is required. No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers are used.

3. Location Data

ClueList uses your device's GPS location only while the app is running or in the background to detect nearby stores. This data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. You can disable location access at any time through your device settings, though this will disable the store proximity notification feature.

4. Third-Party Services

ClueList uses Google Play Services Location API for GPS functionality. This runs entirely on-device. No data is transmitted to any external server.

5. Data Security

Since all data remains on your device, security is managed by Android's built-in app sandboxing and permissions system.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after being posted.

8. Contact Us
