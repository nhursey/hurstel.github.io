Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026
Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Buy Here Check Here. This Privacy Policy explains how Hurstel ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Buy Here Check Here does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data is processed locally on your device.

Data processed locally on your device includes:

  • Images of loan disclosure documents (captured via camera)
  • Loan calculation inputs (amount, interest rate, term, credit score)
  • Calculation results (amortization, equity timeline)

No account creation or sign-in is required. No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers are used.

3. Camera Access

Buy Here Check Here uses your device's camera only when you actively choose to capture a disclosure document. Images are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

4. Third-Party Services

Buy Here Check Here does not use any third-party services that process your data. All processing occurs entirely on your device using standard Android APIs.

5. Data Security

Since all data remains on your device, security is managed by Android's built-in app sandboxing and permissions system. We recommend keeping your device operating system up to date.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
