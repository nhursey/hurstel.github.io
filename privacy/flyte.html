Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026
Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Flyte. This Privacy Policy explains how Hurstel ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Flyte does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers we operate. All data is processed locally on your device.

Data processed locally on your device includes:

  • GPS location (to determine your position for nearby landmarks)
  • Compass and gyroscope data (for AR overlay orientation)
  • OpenStreetMap tile cache (for offline map viewing)
  • POI search history and cached location data

No account creation or sign-in is required. No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers are used.

3. Location & Sensor Data

Flyte uses your device's GPS, compass, and gyroscope sensors to determine your position and orientation for the AR overlay. This data is processed entirely on your device. You can disable location or sensor access through your device settings, though this will limit the app's core functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

Flyte communicates with public OpenStreetMap APIs (Nominatim for geocoding, Overpass API for POI data) to fetch map data and points of interest. These queries include your approximate location. OpenStreetMap's privacy policy applies to those requests. Offline queries do not transmit any data.

Flyte uses Google Play Services (FusedLocationProvider) for location functionality, which operates on-device.

5. Data Security

Since most data remains on your device, security is managed by Android's built-in app sandboxing and permissions system.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
