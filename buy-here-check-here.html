About

Buy Here Check Here helps users understand car loan disclosures through a colorful, engaging interface. Upload TILA disclosure documents, calculate total costs, compare interest rates to credit score averages, and see when you'll build equity — no financial jargon required.

Features

  • Document Upload — Upload TILA disclosure photos and documents
  • Total Cost Calculator — See exactly what you'll pay over the life of the loan
  • Interest Analysis — Compare your rate to credit score averages
  • Amortization Chart — Visual graph of balance over time
  • Equity Timeline — See when you'll be "right side up" on the loan
  • Demo Mode — Test with sample values, no documents needed

Tech Stack

  • Kotlin + Android SDK
  • CameraX for document capture
  • Material Design
  • Charting for amortization and equity visualization
