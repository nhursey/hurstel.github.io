About

Well-Maintained helps you track maintenance tasks, monitor your home's health score, and never miss a deadline. Manage multiple properties, log appliance warranties, and get daily reminders for upcoming and overdue tasks.

Features

  • Home Health Score — Ring gauge showing your home's overall condition
  • Smart Scheduling — Tasks organized by category (HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, etc.)
  • Priority System — High/Medium/Low with overdue alerts
  • Multi-Home Support — Manage multiple properties
  • System Tracking — Log appliances, warranties, and installation dates
  • Cost Tracking — DIY vs professional costs per task
  • Notifications — Daily reminders for upcoming and overdue tasks

Tech Stack

  • Kotlin + Jetpack Compose
  • Material 3 Design
  • Room Database
  • ViewModel + StateFlow
  • WorkManager (background reminders)
  • Navigation Compose
  • DataStore Preferences
